За пределами съёмочной площадки я увлекаюсь цифровым искусством, новыми медиа и держу тело в тонусе. Art, technology and movement define my daily energy. Every role — lead or supporting — gets contemporary sensitivity. I read and write - prose, poetry, and screenplays. Away from the film set, I'm into digital art, new media, and staying physically fit.
